## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one printed circuit board. Project Z's real architecture is the board's main circuit, which a visitor can trace flow by flow. It refuses the category default: a dark hero with a gradient, "Hi, I'm", skill pills and a grid of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: matte green solder mask ground (#0e3b2c, deep #081f17), exposed copper traces (#c8873a, the only warm colour), white silkscreen type (#efece1), gold test pads and edge connectors (#d8b35b). Chakra Petch (Cadson Demak, Bangkok) for silkscreen display and designators; Bai Jamjuree for body text. Chips are dark rectangles with pin rows, labels silkscreened beside them, reference designators (U1, J1, TP3) on everything. Lists are a bill of materials (BOM) table. Square corners, 1px silkscreen outlines, drill holes at the board corners.

STORY: In the first screen the visitor learns who Nawat is and sees the system he architected. Below that come the evidence (test points carrying measured readings, the sprint rail, results), then the other projects as BOM rows, skills, education, and connector J1 to email and résumé.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top board edge: designator "NW-01 REV 2026.11" on the left, nav pins on the right, and a gold "Résumé PDF" edge connector. Left 5 columns: the name in large silkscreen caps, the role line, two connector pads (Email, Résumé). Right 7 columns: the Project Z circuit with 8 chips (Dashboard, Widget, Auth, Chat, Analysis, Redis, Firestore, Gemini) joined by copper traces, plus five flow switches ①–⑤ beneath it.

FORM: Silkscreen Board, position 6 of my ordered list (assigned). seed:0f987783fe9b4876. Signature interaction: choosing a flow sends a pulse of current along the real path, with chips lighting in sequence and traces fading behind (raise from Bioluminescent Wake). Raises: one continuous board with tabular sprint dates on a rail (Daylight Section); copper is the only warm colour (Flash Scrawl); every component has a deep-link designator (HyperCard).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
